Understanding biomolecular dynamics is a key step toward biological intelligence.

Abstract

Molecular recognition, catalysis and biological regulation are shaped by the coordinated conformational dynamics of interacting biomolecules. Learning the joint organization of alternative conformational states and their associated atomic fluctuations remains a central challenge. We present OriDynamic, a unified generative framework for all-atom conformational ensembles of protein monomers, protein–ligand complexes and protein–protein assemblies. OriDynamic integrates global exploration and local exploitation of conformational space through latent ensemble decomposition guided by ensemble-level statistical supervision. Across these systems, OriDynamic improves agreement with reference fluctuation statistics and conformational distributions, while recovering experimentally observed alternative states alongside partner-dependent structural responses, with an extension for sampling configurations associated with ligand dissociation. By enabling systematic exploration of conformational variability, OriDynamic provides a dynamical foundation for AI-driven drug discovery and marks a step toward biological intelligence grounded in the dynamic biomolecular world.

Overview

Highlights

  • Unified multimodal ensemble generation

    A single model generates conformational ensembles across protein monomers, protein–ligand complexes, and protein–protein assemblies.

  • From alternative states to local fluctuations

    Combines latent-space exploration of alternative conformations with conditional all-atom sampling to capture local variability within each state.

  • Ensemble-level learning of conformational variability

    Joint supervision of mean atomic positions, fluctuation amplitudes, and coordinate covariance captures how atoms fluctuate individually and together.

  • Strong ensemble fidelity across benchmarks

    Achieves leading flexibility correlations across four benchmarks, alongside strong agreement with reference conformational distributions and interaction geometry.

  • Functional conformations and partner-dependent responses

    Recovers experimentally observed alternative states and captures shifts in apo–holo conformational distributions in response to ligand or protein partners.
