Abstract

Biomolecular function depends on both alternative structural states and atomic fluctuations within each state. OriDynamic organizes these two levels of variability through latent ensemble decomposition. A structural autoencoder learns a continuous representation, a flow module samples representative conformations across basins, and a conditional refinement module explores all-atom variability within them. Ensemble-level objectives constrain mean positions, fluctuation amplitudes and joint coordinate covariance. A jointly trained model supports protein monomers, protein–ligand complexes and protein–protein assemblies, with evaluation across four public ensemble benchmarks. Application studies examine alternative experimental states and partner-dependent apo and holo conformations. A separately adapted model is evaluated on ligand-dissociation-related configurations. OriDynamic produces time-independent structural ensembles that can support testable hypotheses about molecular recognition and function.

Architecture

Representative states across basins. Atomic fluctuations within them.

1. Structural autoencoder

A continuous structural representation connects molecular conformations to a latent space and reconstructs them as atomic coordinates.

2. Latent flow

Conditioned on molecular context, the flow module generates representative conformations across different structural basins.

3. Conditional all-atom refinement

Refinement samples variability around each representative conformation while improving local atomic geometry.

Ensemble-level supervision

Mean atomic positions, fluctuation amplitudes and trace-normalized joint coordinate covariance guide the generated ensemble. Statistics are evaluated on selected representative atoms: protein Cα and ligand heavy atoms.

OriDynamic generates time-independent structural ensembles. Sample order does not represent physical time, transition rates or a molecular trajectory.

Evaluation details

ConforMix. For each reference state, success requires a sample with TM-score > 0.8 that is closer to that state than to the opposite state. Selected structural illustrations use separate 1,000-sample ensembles.

ProMiSE. The displayed success rate weights clusters equally. Pair-weighted success is 45.4% (ESMFold2: 49.6%). Rankings depend on weighting and the reference-scoring policy described in the manuscript.

Dissociation-related ligand configurations

Evaluation details

Uses a separately adapted model. Precision at 2 Å is 66.3% (BioKinema: 95.5%). Samples describe configurations and distributions, not dissociation trajectories, transition times or rates.
