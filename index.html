Overview

Abstract

Biomolecular function depends on both alternative structural states and atomic fluctuations within each state. OriDynamic organizes these two levels of variability through latent ensemble decomposition. A structural autoencoder learns a continuous representation, a flow module samples representative conformations across basins, and a conditional refinement module explores all-atom variability within them. Ensemble-level objectives constrain mean positions, fluctuation amplitudes and joint coordinate covariance. A jointly trained model supports protein monomers, protein–ligand complexes and protein–protein assemblies, with evaluation across four public ensemble benchmarks. Application studies examine alternative experimental states and partner-dependent apo and holo conformations. A separately adapted model is evaluated on ligand-dissociation-related configurations. OriDynamic produces time-independent structural ensembles that can support testable hypotheses about molecular recognition and function.

Architecture

Representative states across basins. Atomic fluctuations within them.

1. Structural autoencoder

A continuous structural representation connects molecular conformations to a latent space and reconstructs them as atomic coordinates.

2. Latent flow

Conditioned on molecular context, the flow module generates representative conformations across different structural basins.

3. Conditional all-atom refinement

Refinement samples variability around each representative conformation while improving local atomic geometry.

Ensemble-level supervision

Mean atomic positions, fluctuation amplitudes and trace-normalized joint coordinate covariance guide the generated ensemble. Statistics are evaluated on selected representative atoms: protein Cα and ligand heavy atoms.

OriDynamic generates time-independent structural ensembles. Sample order does not represent physical time, transition rates or a molecular trajectory.

Ensemble Performance

Evaluation across protein monomers, protein–ligand complexes and protein–protein assemblies.

The same model is jointly trained on ATLAS, MISATO and mdCATH; DynaPPI evaluates protein–protein assemblies. The values below are manuscript point estimates. Atom selections and sampling budgets differ across benchmarks.

Protein monomers

OriDynamic is evaluated on 82 ATLAS targets and 100 mdCATH targets with 250 predictions per target. Evaluation combines flexibility correlations, ensemble distances and structural diversity.

0.892 ATLAS · 0.847 mdCATH

Median within-target RMSF Pearson correlation, evaluated on protein Cα atoms.

Protein–ligand complexes

Evaluation on 255 complexes, with 100 predictions per target, examines protein and ligand flexibility, intermolecular distances and interaction distributions.

0.695 MISATO

Median within-target RMSF Pearson correlation, evaluated on ligand heavy atoms.

Protein–protein assemblies

Evaluation on 36 complexes, with 100 predictions per target, examines whole-complex and interface fluctuations, contact geometry and distributional coverage.

0.786 DynaPPI

Median within-target RMSF Pearson correlation, evaluated on whole-complex heavy atoms.

Applications

From alternative conformations to molecular interactions.

Alternative experimental states

OriDynamic explores distinct experimentally observed conformations, including domain rearrangements and local structural changes.

64.8% dual-state TM-score success

ConforMix · 59 of 91 targets · 100 samples per target.

Evaluation details

For each reference state, success requires a sample with TM-score > 0.8 that is closer to that state than to the opposite state. Selected structural illustrations use separate 1,000-sample ensembles.

Partner-dependent conformational responses

Comparing apo and holo ensembles reveals how a ligand or protein partner can change the accessible conformational states.

54.7% cluster-weighted dual-state success

ProMiSE · 456 structure pairs in 160 clusters · 100 samples per condition.

Evaluation details

This result weights clusters equally. Pair-weighted success is 45.4% (ESMFold2: 49.6%). Rankings depend on weighting and the reference-scoring policy described in the manuscript.

Dissociation-related ligand configurations

A separately DD-13M-adapted model samples ligand configurations spanning bound and displaced interaction states.

66.0% mean pose coverage at 2 Å

DD-13M · 28 test complexes · 300 samples per target.

Evaluation details

Uses a separately adapted model. Precision at 2 Å is 66.3% (BioKinema: 95.5%). Samples describe configurations and distributions, not dissociation trajectories, transition times or rates.
